# 13 — UiPath Learning Academy Experience

**Product**: ACE UiPath Community Digital Ecosystem  
**Pedagogical Alignment**: UiPath Academic Alliance & UiRPA Certification Blueprint  

---

## 1. Purpose & Learning Philosophy

Provide a self-paced, structured curriculum that takes engineering students from complete automation beginners to certified enterprise RPA architects.

---

## 2. The 4-Track Curriculum Roadmap

1. **Track 1: Citizen Developer (StudioX)**: Zero-code Excel, PDF, email, and web scraping for freshers.
2. **Track 2: Associate RPA Developer (Studio)**: Modern selectors, DataTables, LINQ, Computer Vision, and Orchestrator Queues.
3. **Track 3: Enterprise Automation Architect (REFramework)**: Transactional State Machines, Config.xlsx, Queue Locks, and Auto-Healing Error Handling.
4. **Track 4: Intelligent Automation (AI Center & Document Understanding)**: ML Extractors, Action Center human-in-the-loop, and GenAI activities.

---

## 3. Interactive Lesson Reader UX

Each lesson provides:
* **Pedagogical Overview**: Estimated duration, difficulty level, and primary UiPath product badge.
* **Formatted Markdown Content**: Step-by-step instructions with syntax-highlighted C#/VB expressions and XML selectors.
* **Practice Exercises**: Real-world problem prompts with acceptance criteria.
* **Starter & Solution Workflows**: One-click download of `.xaml` starter templates and solution packages.
