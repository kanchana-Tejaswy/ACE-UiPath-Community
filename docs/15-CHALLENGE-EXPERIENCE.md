# 15 — Challenges & Hackathons Experience

**Product**: ACE UiPath Community Digital Ecosystem  
**Scope**: Competitive Sprints, Hackathons & Hall of Champions  

---

## 1. Purpose

Fuel hands-on automation competition under time constraints, identifying top student talent for industry placements and leadership roles.

---

## 2. Competition Sprints Structure

1. **Flagship Enterprise Hackathon**: 24-48h hackathons with ₹25,000+ prize pools, enterprise problem statements, and industry jury panels.
2. **Monthly Bot Sprints**: 2-week agile challenges testing specific UiPath capabilities (e.g. Modern Selectors, Document Understanding).
3. **Ideation Sprints**: Architecture design and feasibility blueprints.

---

## 3. Challenge Page UX

* **Live Countdown Timers**: Real-time visual countdown to submission deadlines.
* **Dataset Downloads**: One-click download of sample invoice batches, PDFs, or CSV datasets.
* **Evaluation Criteria Rubric**: Clear percentage weightings (Business Value 30%, REFramework Architecture 30%, Exception Handling 20%, Code Cleanliness 20%).
* **Team Submission Form**: Team name, roll numbers, GitHub repo URL, and 3-minute demo video link.
* **Hall of Champions**: Historical leaderboard archiving winning teams from past hackathons.
