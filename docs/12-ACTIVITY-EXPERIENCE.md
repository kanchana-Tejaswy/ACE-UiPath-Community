# 12 — Activity Experience & Institutional Memory Engine

**Product**: ACE UiPath Community Digital Ecosystem  
**Core Asset**: Flagship Institutional Knowledge Preservation Engine  

---

## 1. Timeline & Activity Philosophy

> **Clicking an activity must navigate directly to its dedicated institutional memory page — NOT open a generic popup modal.**

Activities are the backbone of the community's history. When a student clicks an activity on the timeline (e.g. *REFramework Deep-Dive Masterclass*), they are taken to `/activities/reframework-masterclass-2026`, preserving the full context of that workshop as an evergreen educational asset.

---

## 2. Activity Archival Page Breakdown

```text
Activity Memory Page (/activities/[slug])
│
├── 1. Event Identity Header
│   ├── Event Title & Hero Badge (Workshop, Hackathon, Certification)
│   ├── Logistics Bar (Date, Start/End Time, Venue, Delivery Mode, Status)
│   └── Action Bar ("Download Workflows", "Watch Recording", "View Slides")
│
├── 2. The Activity Story
│   ├── Executive Summary
│   ├── Why it was conducted & target business problem
│   └── Technical Deep-Dive Markdown
│
├── 3. Minute-by-Minute Agenda
│   └── Chronological session timeline with speaker assignments
│
├── 4. Speaker Roster
│   └── Industry speakers & student mentors (Avatar, Name, Title, Org, Bio, LinkedIn)
│
├── 5. UiPath Topics Covered
│   └── Topic badges (e.g. Studio 2024.x, Orchestrator Queues, REFramework, State Machines)
│
├── 6. Measurable Learning Outcomes
│   └── Bulleted competencies acquired by attendees
│
├── 7. Media Gallery & Video Lecture
│   ├── Responsive embedded YouTube / Cloud video player
│   └── High-resolution photography lightbox gallery
│
├── 8. Downloadable Artifacts Vault
│   ├── Presentation Slide Deck (PDF)
│   ├── Starter Workflow Package (.XAML / .ZIP)
│   ├── Final Solution Package (.NUPKG / .XAML)
│   └── GitHub Repository Link
│
├── 9. Student Achievements & Badge Awards
│   └── List of hackathon winners or certification recipients
│
└── 10. Connected Knowledge Modules
    ├── Linked Learning Module (Track 3: Enterprise Architect)
    └── Linked Student Bots in Automations Vault
```

## 3. Why This Matters for Student Batches

Four years from now, incoming CSE/IT/ECE freshers can open past activities from 2024 or 2026, study the exact code delivered during those masterclasses, and build upon them without needing senior student leads to be physically present.
