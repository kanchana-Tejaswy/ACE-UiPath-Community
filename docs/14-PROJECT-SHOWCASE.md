# 14 — Automations Vault & Bot Showcase

**Product**: ACE UiPath Community Digital Ecosystem  
**Scope**: Campus Automation Portfolio & Student Bot Showcase  

---

## 1. Purpose

The Automations Vault (`/projects`) serves as the verified portfolio of production-ready software robots engineered by ACE students to eliminate operational drag across college administration, grading, library management, and industry partner projects.

---

## 2. Bot Showcase Inspection Modal

When a user clicks on a bot card, an inspection modal opens displaying:
1. **Header & Tagline**: Title, author attribution (Student Name, Roll Number, Branch), and primary UiPath tools.
2. **Measured Business ROI**: High-visibility ROI metric (*"Saves 45 hours per semester; 100% calculation accuracy across 850 students"*).
3. **Problem vs Solution**: Detailed technical breakdown of the manual bottleneck and UiPath bot architecture.
4. **Artifact Links**: GitHub repo URL, downloadable compiled `.nupkg` package, and video demo stream.
5. **Community Upvoting**: Single-click upvote counter.

---

## 3. Student Bot Submission Pipeline

Students submit their bots via the "Submit Your Bot" modal. Submissions enter a `Pending` queue until reviewed and approved by Core Team members or Admins in `/admin`.
