# 10 — Automations Vault & Projects Showcase

**Product**: ACE UiPath Community Digital Operating System  
**Purpose**: Campus Automation Repository & Student Portfolio Engine  

---

## 1. Projects Showcase Philosophy

> **Software robots must solve real-world problems and deliver measurable return on investment (ROI).**

The Automations Vault (`/projects`) is not a collection of toy tutorials. It is an institutional showcase of production-ready automations engineered by ACE students to eliminate manual operational drag across academic grading, library administration, placement notifications, and finance compliance.

---

## 2. Project Entity Structure & ROI Metrics

Every project record contains:
1. **Identity & Tagline**: Title, one-line value proposition, and primary UiPath tools used.
2. **Problem Statement**: What manual bottleneck existed prior to automation.
3. **Technical Solution Architecture**: How the UiPath bot is structured (e.g. Dispatcher/Performer, REFramework, Action Center).
4. **Measured Business ROI**: Specific metrics (e.g., *Saved 45 hours per exam cycle; 100% grade calculation accuracy across 850 students*).
5. **Technical Artifacts**: Direct links to the GitHub repository, demo video, and downloadable `.nupkg` package.
6. **Author Attribution**: Student name, roll number, academic branch, and LinkedIn profile.
