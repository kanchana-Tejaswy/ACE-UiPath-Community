# 24 — Student Experience & Public Engagement

**Product**: ACE UiPath Community Digital Operating System  
**Audience**: ACE Engineering College Undergraduate Students  

---

## 1. Purpose
Deliver an inspiring, world-class SaaS experience that turns curious students into passionate automation engineers and community leaders.

---

## 2. The 4 Pillars of the Student Experience
1. **Frictionless Exploration**: Browse everything (workshops, code, bots, tutorials) without creating an account.
2. **Pedagogical Clarity**: Master automation step-by-step from Citizen Developer (StudioX) to Enterprise Architect (REFramework).
3. **Portfolio Building**: Showcase real student bots with measured ROI metrics in the public Automations Vault.
4. **Community Recognition**: Earn badges, hackathon awards, and fast-track placement into the chapter Core Team.

---

## 3. Current vs. Planned vs. Future State

### CURRENT (Actually Implemented)
- Complete, responsive public experience across 7 main pages (`/`, `/activities`, `/learn`, `/projects`, `/challenges`, `/resources`, `/about`, `/join`).
- Fast command palette search (`Ctrl+K`).

### PLANNED (Decided & Approved)
- Edge-cached static delivery for instant page loads.
- Student authentication for saving progress and submitting bots.

### FUTURE (Under Consideration)
- Personalized student profile page highlighting attended workshops, certifications, and deployed bots.
