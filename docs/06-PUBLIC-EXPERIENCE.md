# 06 — Public Experience & Student Discovery

**Product**: ACE UiPath Community Digital Operating System  
**Audience**: ACE Engineering College Students, Faculty, Recruiters, and Global Automation Community  

---

## 1. Zero-Friction Public Exploration Principle

> **Curiosity must never encounter an authentication barrier.**

A student discovering the ACE UiPath Community should be able to instantly:
- Explore the complete multi-year activity history (2022 to 2026+).
- Study all 4 learning tracks, read tutorials, and view code/selectors.
- Download starter `.xaml` workflows and production templates.
- Inspect student bots, watch demo videos, and check measured ROI metrics.
- Read about chapter leadership and UiPath Academic Alliance accreditation.

Authentication is introduced strictly when the student decides to take an active identity-based action (submitting a bot, registering a team for a hackathon, or applying for the Core Team).

## 2. Public View Directory & Navigation

```text
/ (Home) 
├── /activities (Activity Timeline & Institutional Memory Archive)
│   └── /activities/[slug] (Deep Activity Record with Agenda, Slides, .XAML, Video)
├── /learn (UiPath Learning Academy)
│   └── /learn/[trackSlug] (Track modules and lesson reader)
├── /projects (Automations Vault & Bot Showcase)
│   └── Modal Inspection (Architecture, ROI, GitHub, .nupkg)
├── /challenges (Hackathons & Competitive Sprints)
│   └── Active Sprint Specs, Datasets & Submission Portal
├── /resources (Templates, Cheat Sheets, Certification Mock Exams)
├── /about (Institutional History, Faculty Advisors, Leadership Wall)
└── /join (Membership, Core Team & Mentor Applications)
```

---

## 3. SEO, Performance & Accessibility Standards

1. **Semantic HTML**: Descriptive `<h1>` to `<h6>` hierarchy, `<main>`, `<nav>`, `<header>`, `<footer>`, `<section>`, and `<article>`.
2. **Metadata & OpenGraph**: Dynamic social preview cards for each activity and student bot (`og:title`, `og:description`, `og:image`).
3. **Keyboard Accessibility**: Full `Tab` navigation, `Escape` to close modals, `Ctrl+K` for instant command palette search.
4. **Performance**: Optimized responsive image formats (WebP/AVIF), CSS variables for zero-runtime styling overhead, and server-cached HTML for instant loading.
